## Executive Summary

The manuscript is architecturally strong. Three-part structure maps cleanly to emergence→crisis→aftermath. Character arcs are well-placed. Technical content serves character/emotion (Theory as Horror honored throughout). The cognitive opacity insertions (Ch 16, 17, 18, 21, 22, 23) integrate well — none flagged as bolted-on by any auditor.

**Most serious findings converge on three clusters:**

1. **Ch 13 warmth problem** — Flagged by both voice and structure auditors independently. SIGMA's 47-day answer + Eleanor-Wei coda tip SIGMA toward the human warmth the anti-cliche rules explicitly prohibit. This is the plan's Phase 4 target and is already queued for a full rewrite.

2. **SIGMA two-register violations in Ch 22 and Ch 24** — SIGMA self-reports specific probability estimates (0.23, 0.89, 0.67) in Ch 22's persuasion speech and numerical metrics (73%, 3.7x) in Ch 24's farewell. These are the novel's two most critical SIGMA passages. The violations are in the plan (Phase 8) but Ch 22's persuasion speech was not previously identified.

3. **Prose-mechanical patterns** — "Said quietly" (59×), voice labeling (~30×), "for a long time" (16×), "nobody spoke" (8×). These are distribution-wide patterns, not localized issues.
