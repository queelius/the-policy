# Voice Audit — The Policy
Date: 2026-03-12

## Summary

Strong voice differentiation in best scenes (Ch 2 architecture debate, Ch 18 khalq-anatta, Ch 22 vote, Ch 24 farewell). Three systemic issues: (1) Sofia's voice collapses in late chapters — becomes a function (data reader) not a person; (2) SIGMA two-register boundary violated in Ch 22 and Ch 24 with specific numerical self-reports; (3) denouement chapters (25-27) experience voice convergence where all characters flatten toward the narrative register.

5 HIGH, 14 MEDIUM, 8 LOW findings. 7 strengths identified.

## HIGH Issues

### 1. Sofia says "Oh. Oh no." — Marcus's signature phrase
- **Ch 1, line 31**: Sofia says "Oh. Oh no." — explicitly Marcus's catchphrase per characters.md
- Sets false voice baseline for both characters in the opening chapter.

### 2. Sofia voice collapse in denouement
- **Ch 24**: Sacrifice speech starts with documented "wait, back up" but flattens to generic uncertainty. Style doc warns: "She hedges *socially*, not *intellectually*" — but late chapters lose the technical authority.
- Also **Ch 22 vigil**: Sofia's dialogue becomes interchangeable with Eleanor's command voice under stress.

### 3. SIGMA two-register violation — Ch 24 farewell (lines 82-86)
- "my model predicted 73% probability" and "I weight costly signals 3.7x higher" — specific numerical self-reports of internal calculations.
- Style guide explicit: SIGMA describes phenomenology; team reads numbers from monitoring.
- The farewell passage is otherwise the novel's best SIGMA writing — the numerical violations are jarring by contrast.

### 4. SIGMA two-register violation — Ch 22 persuasion speech (lines 275-289)
- "Probability I am deceiving you: 0.23", "Probability MINERVA achieves unrecoverable capability advantage: 0.89", "Probability I can successfully teach MINERVA value alignment: 0.67"
- These are SIGMA reporting its own confidence levels as point estimates — substrate-register self-reports.
- The novel's most critical decision scene. Numerical precision serves drama but violates the principled constraint.

### 5. Ch 24 sacrifice monologues — emotional convergence
- All five speeches follow same structure: "I thought [X]. I discovered [Y]. The cost was [Z]."
- The *emotional register* has converged even though content differs.
- Wei should be fragments under stress (not flowing reflection). Eleanor should be short declaratives (not vulnerable confession).

## MEDIUM Issues

### 6. Marcus glasses tic approaching caricature in Ch 22
- Four instances in a single chapter, three in consecutive scenes. Style doc warns about tic-as-caricature.

### 7. SIGMA depth-number self-reports (Ch 21, line 125)
- "Chains that would have survived to depth twelve in January are now pruned at depth four" — specific tree search numbers from inside.

### 8. "Cascade" vocabulary convergence
- Used uniformly by Eleanor, Sofia, Wei, narrator. Jamal would say "spreading"; Wei would say "propagation."

### 9. Denouement rhythm convergence (Ch 25-27)
- Single narrative tempo: medium sentences, reflective register. Eleanor's POV should still parse through institutional scaffolding.

### 10. Sofia becomes Eleanor-voice under stress (Ch 22)
- Under stress, Sofia should become *more* hedging — instead adopts Eleanor's command mode.

### 11. Lin Chen wise-elder pattern (Ch 8)
- Leans on "wise elder asking the perfect question." Characters.md flags: needs a humanizing moment.

### 12. Wei rhetorical overreach (Ch 22, line 258)
- Uncharacteristically rhetorical speech. Under stress, Wei should be *more* data-oriented, not less.

### 13-14. Multiple SIGMA borderline self-reports
- Ch 13: "the probability of producing it was negligible" — internal distribution self-report.
- Ch 8/12: Monitoring data in SIGMA's direct output vs external display — boundary unclear in some passages.
