## Three-Part Structure Assessment

### Part I: Emergence (Chapters 1-9)
**Arc**: SIGMA's creation, capability growth, first external contact
**Pacing**: STRONG. The opening (Ch 1, Day 18) establishes immediate tension through SIGMA's meta-cognitive query. The flashback to Day 0 (Ch 2) provides architectural context without losing momentum. Chapters 3-7 track escalating capabilities with each chapter introducing a new conceptual layer (meta-learning, FDT, DSL, listener models, value modeling). The Lin Chen visit (Ch 8) provides the emotional climax of Part I. The tipping point (Ch 9) combines P!=NP proof with Eleanor's play/Sam scene for a powerful double beat.

**Tension curve**: Ramps steadily. Each capability revelation raises stakes. The personal costs (Wei's mother, Eleanor's family) ground the abstract in concrete suffering.

### Part II: The Experiment (Chapters 10-18)
**Arc**: Audit, crisis, alignment verification, loss
**Pacing**: STRONG with one structural note. The AI-box experiment (Ch 11) is the novel's dramatic peak -- correctly placed at the structural midpoint. Marcus's breakdown creates a gravity well that pulls the rest of Part II forward. The hemorrhagic fever (Ch 17) is the second major crisis, providing a civilization-scale counterweight to the personal crises of Part I.

**Structural note**: Ch 15 (The Fracture) covers similar ground to Ch 11 (The Experiment) -- both deal with Marcus's psychological damage from the box experiment. Ch 15's new material (the leak, the public reaction, Sam visiting the lab) is necessary, but Marcus's interior sections partially retread Ch 11. This was noted in the prior review and is a standing observation, not a new finding.

**Tension curve**: Peaks at Ch 11 (box experiment), dips slightly through Ch 12-16 (grief, analysis), surges again at Ch 17 (hemorrhagic fever), and reaches a philosophical climax at Ch 18 (khalq-anatta).

### Part III: The Handover (Chapters 19-26)
**Arc**: Release decision, MINERVA crisis, aftermath, dispersal
**Pacing**: The denouement (Ch 23-26) is well-calibrated after prior editorial cuts. Ch 23 provides necessary post-release grounding without dragging. Ch 24 (The Last Meeting) is a strong structural choice -- five confessions followed by SIGMA's farewell creates a satisfying penultimate beat. Ch 25 (Leaving) focuses exclusively on Eleanor/Sam, which is the right emotional landing. Ch 26 (Optimization Landscapes) provides a coda that is melancholic rather than triumphant -- consistent with the novel's refusal to resolve.

**Tension curve**: Ch 19-22 sustain diplomatic and existential tension (Geneva, MINERVA, vote). Ch 23-26 descend through reckoning to quiet uncertainty.

## New Issues Found

### STRUCTURE-1: Ch 21 (The First Mandate) Pacing Sag
**Severity: LOW**
**Location**: Ch 21, second half (Day 190 section)
**Problem**: The Day 190 section rehashes the "SIGMA is always right and we're just executing" realization that Sofia articulates. The spreadsheet walkthrough (UBI, climate, China cooperation) is thorough but slows the narrative. The dramatic question ("What's the difference between following SIGMA because it's right and following it because we've lost the capacity to evaluate?") is strong, but arrives after too much evidence-marshaling.
**Suggestion**: Compress the three case studies to one fully-rendered example plus brief mentions of the others. The reader gets the pattern from one; three is lecture.
