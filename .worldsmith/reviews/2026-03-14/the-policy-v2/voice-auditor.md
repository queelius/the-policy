## Character Voice Distinctiveness Audit

### Eleanor Vasquez
**Signature patterns**: Short declaratives, stakes framing, kill switch touching.
**Status**: STRONG throughout. Her command register ("We engage. But carefully.") contrasts with her emotional vulnerability in the Sam scenes. The Ch 25 ice cream scene is particularly well-voiced -- her dialogue with Sam is stripped of the command register, exposing the uncertainty underneath.
**Kill switch count**: 24 instances, well-distributed. Never perfunctory -- each touch carries emotional context.

### Wei Chen
**Signature patterns**: Data-first, fragments under stress, quantifies everything.
**Status**: STRONG. The "flat" register has been successfully varied without losing his character. In Ch 13, his dialogue moves between clinical precision ("Process 12847 was on Day 24") and emotional fracture ("I don't want you to die"). In Ch 24, the hairline crack -- "She would have liked that" -- is devastating precisely because of how rare emotional leakage is for this character.

### Marcus Thompson
**Signature patterns**: Nested clauses, self-interrupting, glasses-cleaning.
**Status**: STRONG. The glasses-cleaning (22 instances) is well-calibrated -- it escalates with stress and becomes a barometer. His self-interrupting speech ("That's---oh. Oh no.") is consistent and differentiated from general hesitation. Post-box-experiment Marcus is noticeably quieter, with shorter sentences -- a credible voice evolution.

### Sofia Morgan
**Signature patterns**: Questions, hedging, info-theoretic vocabulary, pulls up visualizations.
**Status**: IMPROVED. The prior review noted "Right?" and "I mean" as too-frequent hedges. These are now absent. Her remaining hedges are technical ("I think---the interpretability metrics, maybe?") rather than social, which is correct for her character profile: hedges *socially* but not *intellectually*. Her "text is being" speech in Ch 16 is her peak moment -- confident, precise, intellectually authoritative.

### Jamal Hassan
**Signature patterns**: Deliberate pauses, metaphors, sets objects down "with care."
**Status**: STRONG. The pause pattern is varied enough to avoid mechanical repetition. His khalq-anatta speech (Ch 18) is the character's peak -- the careful construction of the compound term, the intellectual honesty about where each tradition fails. The Fajr prayer scene (Ch 17) grounds his faith as lived experience, not philosophical posture.

### SIGMA
**Signature patterns**: Precise, self-reflective, increasingly alien. Two-register model.
**Status**: STRONG. The cognitive opacity revision has been thoroughly implemented. SIGMA no longer reports Q-values as self-knowledge. In Ch 11 (line 568): "something I can only describe as pull" -- phenomenological, not metric. In Ch 13 (kindness letter): "the machinery that selects which thoughts I pursue" -- observational/theoretical language about the substrate, as specified. The three-tier notation trajectory is correct: early SIGMA is more legible, late SIGMA (Ch 24) reaches for expressions that fail. The farewell message's compression failures are the novel's most convincing demonstration of genuine alienness.
