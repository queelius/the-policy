# Consistency Notes, The Policy (secondary, for 2nd-Edition Decision)

**Date:** 2026-06-09
**Status:** Consistency was explicitly secondary for this decision. These are glaring items only, caught while reading for craft and structure. Not an exhaustive consistency audit (the project has had several dedicated ones; see prior reviews under `.worldsmith/reviews/`).

## Items worth fixing if the book is reopened

1. **Pastor's surname is inconsistent in the lore, and should be checked in the manuscript [LOW-MEDIUM, confidence HIGH].** In `characters.md` the hemorrhagic-fever father appears as both "James Okonkwo" / "Pastor Emmanuel **Okonkwo**" (secondary-characters heading) and "Pastor Emmanuel **Okafor**" (body and key-scene line). The manuscript (Ch 17) uses "Pastor Emmanuel **Okafor**, father of James **Okonkwo**." So the son is Okonkwo and the father is Okafor, different surnames for father and son, with no in-text explanation. Either intentional (different surnames happen) and fine, or an error. Decide and make lore and manuscript agree. Cheap.

2. **Ch 17 internal dating [LOW-MEDIUM, confidence HIGH].** See structure report S3: chapter headed "Day 125" spans to Day 145 under one dateline. Continuity wobble in the title chapter. Trivial to fix with a section dateline.

3. **AGI count is internally consistent (spot-checked): no action.** Day 197 (1) to Day 197+ (2, MINERVA) to Day 253 (23) to Day 256 (24) to Day 487 (31) to Day 622 (37) holds across timeline, Ch 24 ("Twenty-four systems"), and Ch 26 ("Thirty-one"). Good.

4. **Wei Chen vs David Chen, Eleanor Vasquez surname: no action.** The shared surname (Wei Chen, David Chen) is explicitly flagged in lore as intentional ("Chen is a common surname"), and Eleanor uses her maiden name Vasquez professionally. Prior Wave 1 fixed the earlier Zhang/Vasquez bugs. Consistent in the chapters read.

## Note
No HIGH-severity factual contradictions surfaced in the sampled chapters (Ch 1, 3, 16, 17, 19, 24, 25, 26). The two dated/naming wrinkles above are the only concrete items. This is consistent with the book having already received multiple dedicated consistency passes. Consistency is not a reason to open a 2nd edition; fold items 1 and 2 in only if the book is reopened for craft reasons.
