## MEDIUM

### The metro-death "design note" tail re-narrates a scene it just dramatized, shanghai, JUDGMENT
- **Location**: line 93.
- **Quote**: "A train can be on time and still be wrong. On time is a servant. It was never meant to be the master, and the day you forget that, it kills a grandmother in the evening rush and shows you a green board and tells you that you did well."
- **Problem**: Lines 87 to 91 fully dramatize the death (the bag in the door, the firmer re-close, "It departed on time," "shows her a green board"). The design-note then restates that scene as a maxim and re-summons its freshest image ("kills a grandmother in the evening rush and shows you a green board"). This is the "dramatize then narrate the lesson" habit the SPEC flags; it also spends the story's most devastating image a second time at lower voltage.
- **Fix**: keep the diegetic principle, "A train can be on time and still be wrong. On time is a servant. It was never meant to be the master." (her voice, an in-world design note engineers quote). Cut or compress the re-illustration tail ("and the day you forget that... you did well"); the scene already carries it.
- **JUDGMENT**: trims authorial-intent prose adjacent to the protected aphorism spine.

## LOW

### 1. Pacific meditation over-scaffolds an earned climax by one sentence, shanghai, JUDGMENT
- **Location**: line 123. Quote: "and she understood that every real thing her life had taught her reduced, in the end, to one question."
- **Problem**: the meditation is largely earned (structural climax; the question "what will you do to the person who cannot keep up with you?" is beautiful). The single connective quoted announces the thesis a beat too plainly, telling the reader convergence is happening rather than letting it happen.
- **Fix (optional)**: trim just the explicit connective; let the motif-list flow straight into the question.

### 2. Final third leans slightly sage-ward, shanghai (Wise Elder, style.md #5)
- **Location**: line 125. Quote: "That was wisdom, and it was so rare that she had met perhaps four people who had it..."
- **Assessment**: the archetype is, on balance, successfully broken (petty rivalry, lost umbrellas, burns rice, can't sing, resents the wheelchair). The closing movements accumulate summative wisdom that leans toward self-mythology, but the same sentence indicts her ("it had still killed a grandmother before she caught it"), pulling it back. Watch-item, no change required.

### 3. Recurring "the search spun him off / that was his variable" labeling, marcuses, low confidence
- **Location**: lines 46, 94, 128.
- **Problem**: several sections name the tested variable in a near-identical construction, occasionally reading as the author labeling the experiment. The section *openers*, by contrast, are varied and excellent. May be part of the machine-register conceit.
- **Fix (optional)**: vary or drop one or two; let behavior imply the axis (as 402,118, 4,096, 847,390 already do).

### 4. Handle-pun creates a momentary parse snag, webox, low confidence
- **Location**: line 269 (posted by `thirteen_hours`): "I've done the arithmetic more times than thirteen\_hours implies."
- **Problem**: a self-referential pun on the handle-as-number, but mid-thread (where commenters address each other by handle) a reader can briefly misread it as a reference to a different commenter before resolving the wink, costing a beat of clarity at an emotionally load-bearing moment.
- **Fix (optional, if intended keep it)**: "more times than the handle implies," or drop the self-reference.
