## MEDIUM

### The metric-is-a-servant lesson is narrated at the close of several movements, pre-empting the finale, shanghai, JUDGMENT
- **Location**: movement 2 (line 37, "That was the year she understood her mother's phrase..."); movement 4 (line 61, "You chose who waited. She had known this since she was six. The only thing her whole education had added was the ability to prove... exactly how much the choosing cost"); reinforced movement 6 (line 93); completed movement 9 (line 125).
- **Problem**: the escalating triad (pump, buses, metro) and the aphorism spine (plant, half-understand, sharpen, prove, complete) are the intended, protected backbone. The issue is that movements 2 and 4 each already carry their turn in-scene (the Xiazhuang woman holding Lin's split hands, line 35; the farebox-recovery speech, line 59) and then *restate* the takeaway as narration. Because the same thesis is named aloud at the close of movements 1, 2, 4, and 6, the movement-9 completion ("Kind was not hard... to be clever and kind at the same time, at scale... That was wisdom," line 125) competes with four close cousins already stated, softening its landing.
- **Reader impact**: an attentive reader registers the escalation of *scale and stakes* (4 villages, 20M, death) but hears the *same sentence-level moral* re-narrated, reading as re-illustration rather than a woman changed anew; the climax arrives partly spent.
- **Fix**: keep the aphorism recurrence and both in-scene turns; trim or compress the narrated summary in movement 4 especially, and consider letting movement 2 end on the woman's hands without the "That was the year she understood" gloss. Trust the escalation to carry the build so movement 9 lands clean.
- **JUDGMENT**: trims lines adjacent to the protected aphorism spine; defer exact scope to author. **Confidence: medium.**
