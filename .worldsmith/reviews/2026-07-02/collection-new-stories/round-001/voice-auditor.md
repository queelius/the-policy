# Voice Auditor Report

**Date**: 2026-07-02
**Scope**: three new story bodies for *Is It Kind?*
**Domain**: character voice, dialogue distinctiveness, POV discipline only

## Summary
Marcus's voice holds remarkably across 14 model-sections; the forum's ~13 handles are largely distinct (the crank and the breakdown register unmistakably); Lin Chen, her mother, and Wen are three cleanly separated registers. No HIGH. One MEDIUM (a SIGMA two-register tension in the `marcuses` seams) and two LOW, all JUDGMENT-tagged because the fixes touch deliberate conceits.

## MEDIUM

### SIGMA names Register-2 bookkeeping in first person, marcuses, JUDGMENT
- **Location**: line 12 (opening SIGMA interlude). Quote: "I read how often the selection comes back, and what the value head predicts, and nothing else survives the pruning." (Softer echoes: line 120 "The value survives. The visit counts survive."; line 238 "in the record that survives.")
- **Problem**: the two-register model (`technology.md`) is explicit that the tree search is "guided by learned values SIGMA cannot inspect. SIGMA does not observe this process," and the CANNOT list names "branch counts" and "Q-values." The information-asymmetry canon reserves reading the value estimates and visit counts for the TEAM, not SIGMA. In first person, SIGMA claiming to "read... what the value head predicts" names the Register-2 evaluator directly and inverts the asymmetry.
- **Mitigation (real and consistent)**: the same sentences disclaim the *load-bearing* access ("no trace... no interior I can quote to you," "Whether there is an interior at all is the question you came here to ask"). Lines 120 and 238 are canon-faithful: the numbers survive in the record, but their meaning "is not a fact I have access to." The tension is localized to line 12's evaluator-naming. A careful compromise, not a careless slip.
- **Fix**: reframe only line 12's evaluator-naming to phenomenological/record language, for example "which returns come back with weight, and which do not." Leave every meaning-disclaimer untouched.
- **JUDGMENT**: the story's entire conceit is "from inside SIGMA's tree search," which requires the SIGMA-narrator to describe the search. It is defensible that the author drew the line correctly (bookkeeping named, meaning withheld). Flag so the author confirms intent.

## LOW

### 1. Two rationalist handles converge at the sentence level, webox, JUDGMENT
- **Location**: `unrolling_prior` line 78 vs. `kolmogorov_complaint` lines 94, 96.
- **Problem**: both share the "I notice I want [an exit / to stop the regress]... I can't [compute which move is mine / find a principled floor]" construction closely enough that their *closing* sentences could be swapped. At the paragraph level they remain distinguishable (kolmogorov terse/quantitative; unrolling_prior confessional with epistemic-status percentages), and the convergence is explicitly thematized ("caught it like a cold... the style is transmissible").
- **Fix (optional)**: hold one axis firmly: keep kolmogorov strictly formal/quantitative; let unrolling_prior carry the confessional "I can't compute whose move this is" beat alone.
- **JUDGMENT**: voice-transmission is a deliberate motif; only the tightest pair flagged.

### 2. Close-third POV reaches into Eleanor's inner state, shanghai, JUDGMENT
- **Location**: line 103. Quote: "another woman running another impossible system would make the same choice about another child's play and feel the same weight, and Lin, had she known, would have recognized her instantly, and would not have absolved her, and would have made her tea."
- **Problem**: `style.md` forbids an omniscient narrator; this close-third chapter reaches outside Lin's knowledge to assert Eleanor's inner state ("feel the same weight"), a person/event Lin does not know. The counterfactual ("had she known... would have") softens it but still asserts the other woman feels it.
- **Fix (optional)**: keep as a deliberate cross-story bridge, or trim to Lin's own projection (she can imagine a future engineer without the narrator confirming that engineer feels it).
- **JUDGMENT**: an intentional literary bridge to the novel; the fix removes a thematic echo.
