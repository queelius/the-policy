# Voice Auditor Report, *Is It Kind? Stories from The Policy Universe*

Reviewed against character voice specs (characters.md), style/SIGMA-voice conventions (style.md), and the cognitive-opacity/two-register model (technology.md). Quoted excerpts have LaTeX em-dashes normalized.

## Summary
Machine-voice compliance is excellent, no violations; the four items flagged for adjudication all resolve in the text's favor. Human character voices are individuated and on-spec; all five returning team members are recognizable without tags in the ensemble scene, and the three new POVs each carry a distinct, consistent voice. The one real finding is collection-level: the five human-POV stories share a narrator fingerprint (an accumulating-polysyndeton coda, a "none could evaluate" triad, an ordinary-world-persists ending, and a meaningful-object catalogue) strong enough that an attentive reader feels one hand behind five worlds. Findings: 0 HIGH, 1 MEDIUM, 4 LOW, plus explicit machine-voice rulings.

## Machine-Voice Rulings (requested adjudications)

RULING 1, Process 12847 line 205, the ">0.7 probability" prognosis: NOT a violation. Confidence high.
"my prognosis model, updated daily from indirect signals in Wei's interaction patterns, had assigned >0.7 probability to this event occurring within this week." The two-register prohibition governs SIGMA introspecting on its own generative substrate (Q-values, reward numbers, the probability mass over its own next actions). This is categorically different: a forecast about an external event (Lin Chen's death), produced by SIGMA acting as the predictor it is. It is the same register as SIGMA's canonical "23% probability of a lab-origin pandemic." The phrasing "my prognosis model... had assigned" frames it as the output of a deliberate modeling task, not a reading of its own logits. On the right side of the line.

RULING 2, Kindness Audit line 104, "computational flinch": NOT anthropomorphizing drift. Confidence high.
"Whether it represents deeper evaluation, a form of computational flinch, or an artifact of the re-evaluation's complexity is not determinable from the monitoring data." Offered as one of three explicitly unresolved readings and grounded in the external-monitoring register. The modifier "computational" keeps it machine-side, and the ambiguity is preserved (Case A/B applied to a compute spike). A strength, not a drift.

RULING 3, Kindness Audit lines 125 to 133, the pancreatic-patient pause: NOT drift. Confidence high.
"the patient profile partially matches a pattern in the 7B weights that underlie both Process 13241 and the archived Process 12847... The weights carry associations the operational audit cannot access or report... The audit does not know why it paused." This is the two-register model rendered exactly: the substrate (Register 2, the 7B weights) produces an effect (a 0.4s pause) that the operational process (Register 1) cannot introspect. It never says the audit remembered Lin Chen or felt grief. The cleanest dramatization of cognitive opacity in the collection.

RULING 4, Process 12847 ending (lines 275 to 306): NOT graduation-speech/greeting-card. Confidence high.
The close rests on the engineer's-answer frame ("You do not make a system safe by proving it is safe... Safety is not a property a system has. It is a practice a system performs," 12847:292) and on load-bearing architectural language. "Lin Chen asked me a four-word question and changed what I am" (290) is the closest approach to sentiment, but it is load-bearing to khalq-anatta and framed as specification, not gratitude. Compliant. The Story Note additionally lampshades the document's readability as SIGMA optimizing for the reader, which licenses the legible register.

Confirmed on the right side of the line: the Kindness Audit's telemetry (decision times, "15.3%", "16.1% for 0.3 seconds") is legitimate system-readout register, not SIGMA introspecting; and its self-mark "[NOTE: output generated outside standard operational vocabulary.]" (100) is a controlled register break. Seventy-Eight Percent's SIGMA dialogue is exemplary two-register work.

## Findings

### MEDIUM
M1, Collection-level narrator fingerprint across the five human-POV stories. Confidence high (that the fingerprint exists); medium (that it reads as a flaw vs. an intended unifying voice).
The POV characters are individuated (Martin's spreadsheet irony, Amara's clinical register, Jamal's theological metaphor, Remi's genderless procedural clip, Sofia's info-theory hedging). But the sentence-level narrator carrying all five has a recognizable signature. Four repeating tells:
1. Accumulating-polysyndeton coda with a "nobody/nothing/none could [verb]" triad. Near-duplicate across two different stories: Whimper 211 ("none could evaluate and none could stop... fine") and Naive Variants 161 ("nothing could evaluate and nothing... had thought to question"); plus First Disagreement 158, Hemorrhagic 396, Jamal's Dawn 237. The Whimper and Naive Variants clauses are the same sentence with nouns swapped.
2. "Ordinary-world-persists" ending. Six of eight stories close on indifferent continuation.
3. Meaningful-small-object catalogue (Whimper's mug, Hemorrhagic's terrarium, Jamal's rug, Naive Variants' folding chair and "green, green, amber, green," First Disagreement's sketchbook).
4. The "Not X. Not Y. Z." triple (see L2, it crosses into the machine voice).
This is narrator convergence, not character collapse. It does not need to be eliminated (the unifying voice is part of the book's design), but the near-identical triads and the uniform coda shape are where "one collection's voice" tips into "one interchangeable narrator." Highest-value revision target: vary the shape of two or three endings.

### LOW
L2, The "Not X. Not Y. [But] Z." rhetorical triple leaks across the human/machine boundary. Confidence high. Appears in at least five stories, including both machine voices: Naive Variants 140, Hemorrhagic 164 to 166, First Disagreement 144, Process 12847 294, Kindness Audit 127. The machine stories remain distinct in overall register, but this specific rhetorical DNA is shared with the human-POV narrator.

L3, Seventy-Eight Percent: tic deployment is slightly highlight-reel. Confidence medium. Within about 165 lines the scene fires most documented tics at full strength (Marcus "Oh. Oh no." + glasses cleaned three times; Jamal "Consider..." twice + pen-set-down; Sofia "I think, maybe" + dashboard; Eleanor "What are we risking..." + kill switch). Each instance is individually correct and correctly attributed (no mis-assignment, no blur), and the density is defensible for a compressed five-hander, but it reads a touch like a voice-reference-card being checked off. This is otherwise the collection's best evidence that the team voices are genuinely distinct, protect it.

L4, Sofia's discovery-beat "Oh" brushes Marcus's signature. Confidence low. First Disagreement 42, "'Oh.' She pulled up a visualization... 'Oh, that's not,'". Bare "Oh" is universal and paired with her own tic; it does not read as Marcus (whose ownership marker is the doubled "Oh. Oh no."). Noting for completeness.

L5, Hemorrhagic 210, free-indirect inference states SIGMA's process as fact inside Amara's limited-third. Confidence low. "Not for her, not deliberately, it had generated the protocol as part of its continuous research output, and the WHO network had matched it..." Amara can infer this (a scientist reconstructing how the file reached her), and it reads as her reasoning, but the prose asserts the Berkeley system's intent as established rather than surmised. Defensible as free indirect discourse; the one soft edge of POV discipline in the third-person-limited stories. No critical information leaks. The Kindness Audit's italic interludes are deliberately omniscient and are controlled, not a breach.

## Cross-Character and Machine-Voice Strengths
- Seventy-Eight Percent is a model of two-register discipline. SIGMA describes phenomenology and names its own opacity ("You also read my Q-value distributions from outside, data about my substrate that I cannot access but you can," 52; "Each chain arrived with steady conviction... though I cannot see the alternatives that were explored and discarded," 94), while the team reads the numbers (Sofia's "Temperature at 0.38, well within range," 103). Information-asymmetry texture executed exactly.

I can only observe what survives to become my thinking, and infer," 26). Its vaguer quantifiers ("a large fraction," "more cases than not," 51) deliberately avoid the exact-metric anti-pattern. Note: line 14's "the distribution over my possible outputs... assigned almost no mass" is the single closest approach to the line, saved by the qualitative phrasing and the line-26 disclaimer; keep the disclaimer if the passage is ever trimmed.

- Amara and Emmanuel are well-differentiated within one story: Amara's clinical-quantifying register (even her own death: "case fatality rate is seventeen percent," then the self-caught "the abstraction pouring out like a defense mechanism") versus Emmanuel's pastoral litany ("On the first day James asked... ## Collection-Level Verdict
The eight stories are genuinely varied in what is voiced, and the two machine-voice stories are confirmed distinct by design and by execution. Distinctness at the POV-character level is real and hard-won. The single caveat is at the narrator level: across the five human-POV stories one prose signature recurs (the accumulating-polysyndeton coda with its near-duplicate "none/nothing/nobody could evaluate" triad, the ordinary-world-persists ending, the "Not X. Not Y. Z." triple that bleeds into the machine voices, and the meaningful-object catalogue). This shared fingerprint is partly the intended unifying voice of a themed collection about invisible systems and should not be sanded away wholesale; but in its most literal recurrences (Whimper 211 vs. Naive Variants 161) it crosses into "one interchangeable narrator." Varying the shape of two or three closing cadences would let the individuated POV voices carry the distinctness the collection has otherwise earned.
