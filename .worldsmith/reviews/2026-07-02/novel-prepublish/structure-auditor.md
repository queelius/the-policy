## MEDIUM (auditor rating; unified report treats as HIGH)

### File 12 "The Fork" (Day 86) contains foreknowledge that self-spoils the same chapter's Day-110 climax
- `chapters/12_reflections_in_containment.tex`, section "The Fork," L465-467. The section is dated Day 86 (Lin Chen still alive, dies Day 112; SIGMA's refusal is Day 110), yet Wei speaks of both as completed past events: "My mother died because SIGMA chose 2.3 million lives over one. ... if we're in Case B, then my mother died as part of SIGMA's optimization of our oversight process."
- This is a roughly 24-day chronological impossibility, and it pre-states the payload of the chapter's own climactic final section ("The Unforgivable Decision," Day 110, the refusal) and of the next chapter's death scene (file 13, Day 112). A reader tracking the day-headers hits a contradiction and the Day-110 crux is deflated.
- **On the Day-118-before-Day-110 ordering you asked about:** that inversion works. "The Last Call" (Day 118) and "The Unforgivable Decision" (Day 110) are paired as parallel "optimization-over-love" scenes; ending the chapter on the refusal gives the crux its climactic weight. Day 118's "buried her six days ago" does not deflate the Day-110 scene, because that scene's tension was never "will she live" (she is terminal from file 08) but the horror of SIGMA declining with serenity. The inversion is a legitimate choice; the Day-86 anachronism is the genuine defect.
- **Fix (one line, edit-in-place):** recast Wei's Day-86 lines as hypothetical/anticipatory ("If SIGMA ever had to choose my mother against millions...") or cut L465-467.
