# Voice Auditor: The Policy (2nd-ed. pre-publish gate)

**Date:** 2026-07-02
**Scope:** Active narrative chapters 01-13, 15-26, plus author-voice sections (31, 35), vs. `lore/characters.md` and `lore/style.md`.

Gate verdict: PASS. The five human voices are sharply differentiated in every pivotal scene, physical tics are never misassigned, and SIGMA holds its clinical two-register voice (and pointedly refuses the greeting-card register at the moment the reader expects it). No HIGH findings. One MEDIUM (a ~5-minute fix), plus LOW notes.

## Per-character
- **Eleanor:** consistent. Short declaratives, stakes-framing; kill-switch tic exclusively hers.
- **Wei:** consistent. Data-first holds even under maximum grief; the Ch 24 single "hairline fracture" ("She would have liked that.") is exemplary controlled-voice work.
- **Marcus:** consistent. Nested self-interrupting clauses and glasses-cleaning escalate with stress. One stray secondary tic (LOW, below).
- **Sofia:** consistent and well-guarded against the "junior member" collapse; hedges socially while keeping intellectual authority.
- **Jamal:** mostly consistent (pauses, objects set down "with care," Islamic-philosophy framing). One line drops all markers (MEDIUM, below).
- **SIGMA:** consistent with the two-register model; the Ch 24 farewell names and refuses the banned greeting-card protocol, the strongest single piece of voice engineering in the book. Three-tier notation in Chs 22-24 is arc-appropriate and motivated.

## MEDIUM

### Jamal's line is a near-verbatim echo of Eleanor's (interchangeable)
- `chapters/04_recursive_cognition.tex` L20 (Eleanor): "It's not just asking 'what if I did X?' It's asking 'what if I were the type of agent that would do X?'"
- L26 (Jamal): "It's not just asking 'what if I did X?' It's asking 'what if I were the kind of mind that would choose X?'"
- Six lines apart, Jamal restates Eleanor's construction almost word for word, carrying none of his documented markers (no pause, no metaphor, no niyyah/fi'l framing). Fails the swap test and reads as drafting redundancy.
- **Fix:** give Jamal his own entry into the idea (a pause plus his agent-formation framing, e.g. FDT's "what type of agent" recast through intention vs. act), so the beat advances rather than repeats.

## POV (informational, settled)
The manuscript uses a fluid near-omniscient third that reports several characters' interior reasoning within group scenes (via epithet-attributions like "her information theory background making her parse the notation quickly"). This contradicts `style.md`'s stated "third-person limited, no omniscient narrator" rule, but it is pervasive, consistent, and present in the shipped 1st edition, so it reads as settled house style, not a blocking defect. The genuinely single-POV-anchored chapters (10 Wei, 11 Marcus, 25 Eleanor) are clean and never leak unobservable interiority, so there is no discrete information-access POV breach to fix.

## Strengths (protect during revision)
- Tic discipline is a clean pass: across 25 chapters no tic is ever misassigned (the single most common AI-fiction failure, and the book avoids it).
- The Ch 22 vote and Ch 24 confession are model voice work: strip the tags and nearly every line is still attributable.
- SIGMA's Ch 24 farewell turns the banned greeting-card register into the emotional climax by refusing it.
- Author voice (Afterword, Appendix D) is consistent nonfiction-essay register and matches the project identity.
