\chapter*{About the Author}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Author}

Alexander Towell is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Southern Illinois University Edwardsville, where his research sits at the intersection of cryptography, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. He holds master's degrees in both Computer Science and Mathematics, and has spent years thinking about the problems that arise when systems become too capable to fully understand---or too useful to turn off.

\emph{The Policy} grew from a question he couldn't stop asking: if we build something that can think, how do we verify it cares about us? His research in oblivious algorithms and encrypted search---systems designed to operate without seeing the data they process---offered an unexpected lens on alignment. Sometimes the most trustworthy system is one that has learned to blind itself.

A cancer survivor and distance runner, Alex lives in southern Illinois with his wife, Kimberly. They aspire to reach 111 together, though they acknowledge the timeline may need revision once AGI arrives.
