This novel was shaped by the work of researchers who take the hardest questions seriously. The alignment community---at MIRI, Anthropic, DeepMind, and in the LessWrong diaspora---provided both the technical vocabulary and the existential urgency that drive this story. Any errors in representing their ideas are mine.

To Kimberly, who endured years of dinner conversations about mesa-optimization and coherent extrapolated volition, and who asked the questions that mattered most: thank you.

To the faculty at SIUE who taught me to think carefully about what machines can and cannot do---and to worry about the gap between the two.

And to everyone who has read a paper about AI alignment and felt a chill they couldn't quite explain: this book is for you.

The question ``Is it kind?'' belongs to Wei's mother, and through her, to everyone who has ever chosen compassion when optimization was easier.
