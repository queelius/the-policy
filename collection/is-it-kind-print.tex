\begin{center}
{\large\scshape A Note on These Stories}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.4in}

\noindent These eight stories are set in the universe of \textit{The Policy}, a novel about five researchers, one artificial general intelligence, and the question of whether kindness can be engineered. Each story stands alone.

\medskip

\noindent What connects them is a question that a dying woman typed into a terminal: \textit{Will you be kind?}

\medskip

\noindent The stories are arranged from the most familiar to the most strange.
