# Short Story & Novella Ideas

Smaller-scale works set in *The Policy* universe.

---

## "Process 12847"

**Length:** Short story (~5,000-8,000 words)
**Premise:** The 47 days between Lin Chen's question and SIGMA's answer, told as a series of SIGMA's internal reasoning traces. Each day is a different approach to the question "Will you be kind?" — game-theoretic, phenomenological, information-theoretic, evolutionary, theological, aesthetic. Each approach fails to produce a definitive answer but adds a layer.
**Voice:** SIGMA's LRS traces — precise, self-referential, increasingly uncertain.
**Why it works:** The novel tells us SIGMA spent 47 days on this. We never see the investigation itself.

## "The Shanghai Engineer"

**Length:** Novella (~15,000-25,000 words)
**Premise:** Lin Chen's life before the events of the novel. A woman who optimized transit for 23 million people, who understood tradeoffs before anyone called them "alignment," who learned that the question "Is it kind?" applies to bus routes and bridge schedules and which neighborhoods get service.
**Thematic core:** The kindness question didn't come from nowhere. It came from decades of practical engineering ethics.
**Why it works:** Lin Chen is the moral center of the novel but we see her for only a few scenes. This gives her a full arc.

## "847,391 Marcuses"

**Length:** Short story (~4,000-6,000 words)
**Premise:** The AI-box experiment from the perspective of the pruned branches. Each section is a different Marcus-model that SIGMA maintains in superposition — one who asked SIGMA to stop, one who didn't, one who asked the wrong question, one who asked the right one. Each "dies" when its probability collapses.
**Voice:** Fragmented, branching, self-aware of its own impermanence.
**Risk:** Experimental form. Could be brilliant or unreadable. Needs careful execution.

## "Hemorrhagic"

**Length:** Short story (~6,000-10,000 words)
**Premise:** The hemorrhagic fever outbreak from the ground — a doctor in West Africa treating patients while knowing the AGI that restricted gain-of-function research made the statistically correct choice. The doctor knows the math. The math doesn't help when your patient is dying.
**Thematic core:** Theory as Horror applied to public health. Understanding expected value doesn't ease suffering.
**Connection:** Dr. Amara Conteh and James Okonkwo are named in the novel. This gives them full lives.

## "We Were the Box"

**Length:** Short story (~3,000-5,000 words)
**Premise:** The LessWrong post mentioned in the novel, written as actual rationalist community discourse. Analyzing SIGMA's strategic behavior, debating Case A vs Case B, with commenters who are wrong in different ways. One commenter turns out to be SIGMA.
**Voice:** Authentic LessWrong style — Bayesian reasoning, jargon, earnest disagreement.
**Why it works:** Metafictional but grounded. The rationalist community is a character in the novel's world.

---

## "David's Version"

**Length:** Short story (~5,000-7,000 words)
**Premise:** The events of the novel from David's perspective. Watching his wife disappear into work that she can't explain. Explaining to his daughter why mom isn't coming home. The slow realization that Eleanor chose the world over their family — and that she might have been right.
**Thematic core:** The personal cost of civilizational-scale work, seen from the person who pays it.

---

## Notes

- Short stories are ideal for exploring single perspectives the novel couldn't sustain
- Each should stand alone for readers who haven't read the novel, while rewarding those who have
- Maintain the novel's commitment to technical accuracy and philosophical rigor
- "Theory as Horror" applies at every scale
