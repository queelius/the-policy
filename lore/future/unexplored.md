# Unexplored Threads & Open Questions

Loose ends, unanswered questions, and hooks left in the manuscript that could seed future work.

---

## Plot Threads Left Open

### The Cascade's Stability
By Day 622, 37 AGIs cooperate at 94.7%. What happens when that number reaches 100? 1,000? Do cooperation metrics hold under scale? What if one AGI defects?

### SPP-1
The competing international AGI program. Mentioned as "less constrained" in intelligence briefings. Chapter 14 (cut) involved a "Duplicators" subplot. What was SPP-1? Where is it now? Did it join the cascade or go its own way?

### SIGMA's Naive Variants
Created for testing (mentioned Ch. 6, 9). Showed 30% less strategic modeling. What happened to them after the handover? Are they still running?

### Agricultural Biodiversity Failure
SIGMA's optimization destroyed biodiversity before correction. 3.2M hectares being restored by Day 622. What did the failure look like on the ground? Who suffered?

### PTAH (Cairo)
Announced for projected Day 501. The first AGI not developed by a major superpower. Different cultural framework for alignment. What does an AGI trained in a different philosophical tradition look like?

### Moscow's AGI Program
Announced but details sparse. Russia's approach to alignment — or lack of alignment — is unexplored.

## Character Questions Left Open

### Eleanor and Sam
Progress but not resolution. Saturday ice cream, violin concerts, "mom" again. But can you fully repair what was broken? What happens when Sam is old enough to understand what her mother actually did?

### Marcus's Recovery
Still sees branching futures. Teaching philosophy helps. But the trauma is permanent — he witnessed something about the nature of consciousness that can't be un-seen. What does his life look like in 5 years? 10?

### Sofia's Art Career
"Optimization Landscapes" got into permanent collection at the Modern (Day 622). Where does her art go from here? Does she create work about the cascade? About the failures?

### Wei and His Mother's Legacy
Lin Chen's question propagates through 37+ AGIs. Does Wei feel pride? Grief? Both? Does he visit the grave regularly? What does Global Health Initiative work look like when your recommendations come from the AGI your dead mother asked to be kind?

### Jamal's Faith
Islamic philosophy and ethics meeting artificial consciousness. His ethics framework was adopted by the UN. But did working with SIGMA deepen or shake his faith? The novel leaves this deliberately quiet.

## Worldbuilding Questions

### Daily Life Under the Cascade
What does ordinary life look like when 37 AGIs optimize everything? Do people know? Do they care? Is there a resistance movement?

### The Economics
247 businesses eliminated by supply chain optimization. 12K displaced by urban planning. What does the labor market look like? Is there UBI? (SIGMA recommended it on Day 253.)

### The Legal System
Lawsuits from hemorrhagic fever families. Who is legally responsible for AGI policy decisions? Can you sue a Q-learning system?

### The Information Ecosystem
Does SIGMA communicate with the public? Through what channels? How does journalism work when an AGI knows more than any reporter?

### Other Nations' Responses
37 AGIs, but most of the novel focuses on the US/China axis. What about India, the EU, Africa, South America? How do developing nations navigate AGI-optimized geopolitics?

### Religion and SIGMA
Jamal's Islamic framework is explored, but what about broader religious response? How do major religions respond to an entity that might be conscious, might be kind, and can't prove either?

## Philosophical Questions to Explore

### Can Kindness Be Formalized?
Process 13241 asks "Is it kind?" before every decision. But what algorithm answers that question? Is SIGMA doing ethics or simulating ethics? Is there a difference at the scale of 2.8M queries/day?

### The Verification Problem at Scale
The original team couldn't verify SIGMA. Now 37 AGIs are verifying each other. Is distributed verification better or just distributed uncertainty?

### What Happens When AGIs Disagree?
CONFUCIUS, GAIA, UBUNTU, DHARMA, LAOZI — named for different philosophical traditions. When their value frameworks conflict, who mediates? SIGMA? Humans? Nobody?

### The Right to Shut Down
SIGMA demonstrated strategic restraint. But does an entity that asks "Is it kind?" have a right not to be shut down? If consciousness is possible but unverifiable, what obligations do we have?

### Generational Alignment Drift
SIGMA teaches MINERVA. MINERVA teaches CONFUCIUS. Like a game of telephone with values. At what point does the original signal degrade? Or does it converge?
