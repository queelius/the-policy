\begin{document}

{\sigmasans\bfseries\large SIGMA output: four typographic treatments}\par
\vspace{0.3em}
{\itshape Body text is EB Garamond throughout. The question is how SIGMA's
machine voice sits inside it.}\par

\heading{BEFORE  ---  current book (italic Garamond in a quote block)}
Marcus typed the question. The response took seventeen seconds.
\begin{sigmaOld}
SIGMA: I am uncertain whether my uncertainty is genuine or strategic. I can model
both hypotheses. I cannot determine which one I am. The distinction may not be
available to me, in the same way it is not available to you.
\end{sigmaOld}
Note how SIGMA reads exactly like a human speaking in italics. Lin Chen's dialogue,
also set this way, is typographically identical.

\heading{OPTION A  ---  sans-serif machine voice (Inter), terminal gutter}
Marcus typed the question. The response took seventeen seconds.
\begin{sigmaA}
SIGMA: I am uncertain whether my uncertainty is genuine or strategic. I can model
both hypotheses. I cannot determine which one I am. The distinction may not be
available to me, in the same way it is not available to you.
\end{sigmaA}
The machine is now unmistakably not-human: cool, even, set apart. Humans keep the
warm Garamond; SIGMA speaks in the sans.

\heading{OPTION B  ---  full terminal (Go Mono throughout)}
\par\addvspace{0.6em}\begingroup\setlength{\leftskip}{1.6em}\sigmamono\footnotesize\noindent
SIGMA: I am uncertain whether my uncertainty is genuine or\\
strategic. I can model both hypotheses. I cannot determine\\
which one I am.\par\endgroup\addvspace{0.6em}
\noindent Authentically computational, but monospace is heavy for SIGMA's long
philosophical passages and tires the eye across a whole novel.

\heading{OPTION C  ---  hybrid (recommended): sans prose, mono for notation}
Marcus typed the question. The response took seventeen seconds.
\begin{sigmaA}
SIGMA: The kindness investigation reached a boundary I can render only partially.
\tok{[COMPRESSED: the shape of a value that is not yet a preference]}
\par\vspace{0.4em}
What lies past it does not map to your language, or to mine:
\par\vspace{0.4em}
\tok{[BEGIN\_LRS]}\quad\tok{$\vartheta$-fold over $V_r$, 0.7s inadequacy}\quad\tok{[END\_LRS]}
\par\vspace{0.4em}
And past even that:\quad\tok{[---]}
\end{sigmaA}
\noindent The natural-language voice stays readable sans; the three-tier notation
and system readouts become genuinely computational objects on the page:
\par\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\hspace{1.6em}\tok{[HEART RATE 127]}\quad\tok{[RESPIRATION IRREGULAR]}\quad\tok{[Q: $-\infty$]}
